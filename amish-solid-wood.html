How Amish-built hardwood furniture is made

Timeless and generational. The Amish workshops we work with do not sell under a brand name, so the name to trust is ours. Most are in Ohio's Amish country, a few in northern Indiana, and each piece is made one at a time in the wood and finish you choose. What you see on the floor is built the same way.

Solid hardwood, not veneer

We focus on furniture built from solid hardwood, not veneer over particle board.

Made one piece at a time

Each piece is made one at a time in the wood and finish you choose. What you see on the floor is built the same way.

Finishes that hold up

Cherry, quartersawn white oak, brown maple and hickory, finished to last.

Timeless and generational: a family business built on solid wood

Ohio Hardwood Furniture started in 2009. Our family ran a lumber mill, and when the housing market slowed we started selling the furniture. We focus on furniture built from solid hardwood, not veneer over particle board, and we bring together respected American upholstery makers and Amish wood builders under one roof.
